Method

Loading…

  • Drag a size slider (down to half size) and let go: the image is carved again, all vertical seams first, then all horizontal seams.
  • The seams are found by the same C++ as the command-line program, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing is precomputed.
  • Graph Cut is only offered for images of up to 20,000 pixels (the small photos), because it is very slow.
  • Keys: Space replays or pauses, the left and right arrows step one seam.
